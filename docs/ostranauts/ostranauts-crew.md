## 1 Overview
- Crew = NPC recruits. Important for running larger ships and for efficient scavenging and building.
- Extra crew can cover skills the player character lacks.

## 2 Hiring Crew
- Hiring is a **Negotiation** (social combat): get the NPC's **Moodlets into the success state**, like other **Gambit** social moves (wiki: Combat_(Social)).
- During hiring the player has **extra Social Moves** available to help with this.
- Once the moodlets are in success state → make an **offer of employment**. If accepted, the NPC becomes a full crewmember.
- Three terms to negotiate:

| Term | What it is | Range / start |
|---|---|---|
| Salary | **Daily** pay rate; can be raised or lowered in negotiation | Usually **$200–500 per day** |
| Signing Bonus | One-time payment for joining; can be raised or lowered | Always **starts at $0** |
| Death Pay | Up-front insurance payment held in trust | **$10,000–100,000** |

### 2.1 Salary
Daily pay rate, usually $200–500, adjustable during negotiation.

### 2.2 Signing Bonus
One-time bonus for joining, adjustable, starts at $0 every negotiation.

### 2.3 Death Pay
- Paid up front and held in trust.
- Crewmember **quits or is fired** → amount is **returned** to the hiring party.
- Crewmember **dies** → paid to their nominated parties, **not returned**.
- Range $10,000–100,000.

## 3 Controlling Crewmembers
- Switch to a crewmember to control them directly (default: **Caps Lock** or **left-click on the crewmember**).
- **Work shift** (Roster): fully controllable like the player character.
- **Free and Sleep shifts:** obey some orders, but **won't do "work" tasks** such as repairing.
- On **Automatic Behavior** during a Work shift, crew complete **Orders** like the player character where possible.
- Behavior issues: see the wiki FAQ checklist "Why is My Crewmember Not Doing What I Want?" (Help:Frequently_Asked_Questions).
