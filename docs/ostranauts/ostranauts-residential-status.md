# Ostranauts – Residential Status (Reference Memory)

Source: Ostranauts wiki, "Residential Status" page. Section numbering follows the wiki's table of contents. Game terms kept in English.
Wiki base: https://ostranauts.wiki.gg/wiki/
Related: ostranauts-trading.md (5 Bulk Trading), ostranauts-flying.md (4 Locale Navigation, torch travel), ostranauts-character-creation.md (skills).

## Overview
- Residential status = where a person lives and their place in society.
  - Where someone grows up influences their job.
  - Residential status / faction heavily affects **initial relationships with strangers**.
- **Legal status** = recognition by a location's government. Two ways to become a citizen:
  - by **birthright**
  - by earning or purchasing a **visa**: for skilled workers, entrepreneurs, or people sponsored by a citizen/organization. Visas can become hard or impossible to get due to demand.
- Visas are reviewed and bought at an **Embassy Services Kiosk** (on most stations).
- **Benefits of citizenship:** **discounts on Bulk Trading** and the right to **buy a home** there.

## 1 Visa Types and Acquisition
- The player **starts as an illegal occupant of K-Leg** (corporate-owned station on asteroid 1036 Ganymed).
- Legal status = holding a **valid visa** issued by a government or corporation after applying and meeting its conditions.
- NPCs can have various legal statuses depending on origin.
- Not every visa type is offered by every faction.

Quick comparison (cheapest → most expensive):
| Visa type | Cost | Main requirement |
|---|---|---|
| National Service | $250,000 | Operational Service in the Ceres warzone (OSman status) |
| Local Sponsored | $500,000 | Sponsorship from a friendly resident of that faction |
| Skilled Labor | $750,000 | At least one listed skill |
| Authority Sponsored | $1,000,000 | Sponsorship from a friendly law enforcement officer of that faction |
| Forged Skilled Labor | $1,000,000 | Listed skill + criminal contact (Encantado/Newcal only) |
| Investor | $5,000,000–10,000,000 | Fee only |

### 1.2 Skilled Labor Visa
One-time fee + at least one desired skill.
| Issuing Faction | Legal Jurisdiction | Cost | Valid Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encantado | Encantado | $750,000 | Civil Engineering, Construction, Electrical Engineering |
| Ayotimiwa Ship Breaking Co. | K-Leg | $750,000 | Administration (= Business Admin?), Civil Engineering, Construction, Spaceship Engineering |
| Newcal | New California | $750,000 | Civil Engineering, Construction, Electrical Engineering |
| Galilean Confederacy | Fort Simpson, Port Mojave | $750,000 | Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Excavation |
| CCRE | Ring Station | $750,000 | Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Excavation |

Tip (derived): **Civil Engineering** (3 years in chargen) qualifies for every Skilled Labor Visa; **Spaceship Engineering** (a starting skill) qualifies for K-Leg.

### 1.3 Forged Skilled Labor Visa
- For the Venusian aerostats **Encantado** and **New California**. Same function and similar requirements as the real visa, but obtained through **criminal sources**.
- Advantage: obtainable **on the aerostat itself** → no trip to the K-Leg embassies needed.

| Issuing Faction | Jurisdiction | Criminal contact location | Cost | Valid Skills |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Encantado | Encantado | TBD | $1,000,000 | Gambling, Intrusion, Piloting, Stealth, Street Etiquette |
| Newcal | New California | TBD | $1,000,000 | Acting, Cooking, Dancing (= Dance), Hacking, Oligarch Etiquette, Persuasion |

### 1.4 Authority Sponsored Visa
One-time fee + sponsorship from a **law enforcement officer** of the issuing faction. Talk to an LEO on good terms with you and **persuade** them to vouch for you.
| Issuing Faction | Jurisdiction | Cost | Valid Sponsorship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encantado | Encantado | $1,000,000 | Any LEO of the **Polícia Federal** |
| Newcal | New California | $1,000,000 | Any LEO of the **Newcal Police Dept.** |

### 1.5 Local Sponsored Visa
One-time fee + sponsorship from **another visa holder** of that jurisdiction. Persuade a friend or family member on good terms to vouch for you.
| Issuing Faction | Jurisdiction | Cost | Valid Sponsorship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encantado | Encantado | $500,000 | Any friendly Encantado resident |
| Newcal | New California | $500,000 | Any friendly Newcal resident |
| Galilean Confederacy | Fort Simpson, Port Mojave | $500,000 | Any friendly Galilean Confederacy resident |
| CCRE | Ring Station | $500,000 | Any friendly CCRE resident |

### 1.6 National Service Visa
Cheapest, but requires **Operational Service** (military activity) in the contested **Ceres warzone** for the issuing faction. After earning **Operational Serviceman (OSman)** status you may buy the visa.
| Issuing Faction | Jurisdiction | Cost | Operational Service |
|---|---|---|---|
| Galilean Confederacy | Fort Simpson, Port Mojave | $250,000 | Mine asteroids, lay claim beacons, or destroy **CCRE** ships |
| CCRE | Ring Station | $250,000 | Mine asteroids, lay claim beacons, or destroy **Galilean Confederacy** ships |

## 2 Embassy Services Kiosk Locations
- Apply at an Embassy Services kiosk **of the relevant faction**. Not all ports have them, and each port only hosts a subset of factions → getting residency for one port may need **torch travel** elsewhere.
- "Processes applications?": Yes / No as on the wiki; "–" = not stated on the wiki.

| Port (code) | Factions with kiosks → processes visa applications? |
|---|---|
| Encantado (VENC) | Encantado: No · K-Leg (Ayotimiwa): **Yes** · Newcal: No |
| Fort Simpson (JFTS) | Galilean Confederacy: – · K-Leg (Ayotimiwa): **Yes** · Newcal: **Yes** · Titan: **Yes** |
| Hangzhou Orbital (MHNG) | CCRE, Encantado, Galilean Confederacy, K-Leg (Ayotimiwa), Newcal, Suzhou Orbital, Tharsis Landing (Xinhua), Titan, Voltaire: – |
| Jade Rabbit (EJDR) | Encantado: No · K-Leg (Ayotimiwa): **Yes** · Newcal: No |
| K-Leg (OKLG) | CCRE: **Yes** · Encantado: **Yes** · K-Leg (Ayotimiwa): No · Newcal: **Yes** |
| New California (VNCA) | Encantado: No · K-Leg (Ayotimiwa): **Yes** · Newcal: No |
| Port Mojave (BCER) | CCRE, Encantado, K-Leg (Ayotimiwa), Newcal: – |
| Ring Station (BCRS) | CCRE, Encantado, K-Leg (Ayotimiwa), Newcal: – |
| Suzhou Orbital (MSUZ) | CCRE, Encantado, Galilean Confederacy, K-Leg (Ayotimiwa), Newcal, Suzhou Orbital, Tharsis Landing (Xinhua), Titan, Voltaire: – |

Notes (derived):
- Encantado and Newcal visas are processed at **K-Leg (OKLG)**, not on the Venus aerostats themselves (hence the value of forged visas there).
- Odd but as listed: the K-Leg (Ayotimiwa) kiosk at OKLG says "No", while K-Leg (Ayotimiwa) kiosks at many other ports say "Yes".

## 3 Home Ownership
- Bought at the local **Real Estate Kiosk**; you must be a **legal citizen of that location**.
- Cost **$5,000,000**. Includes **unlimited power** and a **dedicated docking port**.
- Great for storing valuables; the private dock makes ship renovation and storage safer and more convenient.

| Fort Simpson Homeowner | Fort Simpson, Ganymede | Galilean Confederacy |

| Newcal Homeowner | Newcal aerostat, Venus | Newcal |
| Port Mojave Homeowner | Port Mojave, Ceres | Galilean Confederacy |
