## 1 Overview
- Salvaging **derelicts** (for profit or to reuse old tech) is a main gameplay pillar.
- Most derelict breaking happens in the **Ayotimiwa Shipbreaker's Yards**; private salvagers pick over low-priority derelicts in the **OKLG Boneyard**.
- Sell salvage at **Kiosks**; rumors of a **Black Market** (Fixer) somewhere on OKLG station.
- Every dockable derelict can be fully stripped, **except the main airlock/docking port** (cannot be modified).
- Ruthless players can also take ships that haven't been abandoned yet…

## 2 Salvage Permit
> "An Ayotimiwa Ship Breaking Co. Salvage Permit. Unexpired permits allow scavengers to salvage derelicts in the OKLG Boneyard without interference from Ayotimiwa Security." ― Ostranauts
- Bought at the **License Kiosk** for **$5,000**; valid for **one full day** of legal salvaging in OKLG local space.
- Also unlocks the License Kiosk's **Barter Screen**.
- Without a permit, docking to a derelict may trigger a docking request and **fine** from **Nnamdi Azikiwe Security Services (NASS)**.
- Permits are **linked to the ship's Transponder** and can be **scanned at range**.
- Longer and cheaper-per-day permit tiers (Red 72 h, Blue 7 d, Silver 30 d, Black 300 d): see ostranauts-trading.md 2.3.

## 3 Salvaging

### 3.1 Checklist
- **Salvage Permit**
- **Tools:**
  | Tool type | Purpose | Options |
  |---|---|---|
  | Structure Cutter | Cut apart walls and floors | Horang "PlastHak" Hacksaw, Gott Angle Grinder, Weber 'Lance' Laser Torch |
  | Welding tool | Repair items, assemble walls/floors | Halvorson Friction Stir Welder, Weber 'Lance' Laser Torch |
  | Soldering tool | Conduits, general electrical items | Gott Soldering Iron, Weber 'Lance' Laser Torch |
  | Screwdriver | Uninstall most equipment | Horang "PlaScrew" Screwdriver, Gott Power Drill |
  | Pry tool | Open locked crates and unlocked-but-unpowered doors | Weber 'Prize' Crowbar (main option); many weapons also work |
- **Space Suit:** Pressure Suit + light (Phoebus Work Lamp or Renbao "Portal" PDA), or EVA Suit (built-in light).
- **Storage space:**
  - No space? Take floors and containers from derelicts to expand your ship.
  - **Racks** effectively **triple** floor space; exterior **Cargo Webbing** = cheap storage outside the hull for less valuable items.
  - **Testudo "SuperHandy" Equipment Truck** holds several heavy items → moves salvage faster.
- **Life support:** keep at least a small space with safe atmosphere to fall back to. Docking at a station costs time and sometimes money; dying costs everything.
  - Food, water, toilet, hygiene supplies → stay out **all day**.
  - Place to sleep + multiple batteries with proper power management → **days**.
  - Reactor → **weeks**.
- **Weapon:** derelicts can hold extra dangers.

### 3.2 Salvaging Tips
- Four basic steps: **survey → uninstall → haul → repair**.
- Early: uninstall and haul manually one item at a time until the hold is full. Later (bigger ship, better tools, crew): queue tasks via **Orders** / AutoTask.
- Crew can get a suit and help on the derelict, or stay aboard and **repair** what you bring back.
- **Damaged** and **Worn** items sell for less:
  - Damaged → repair with **Components**.
  - Worn → fix with **time and work**.
- Derelicts often have the needed repair parts lying around on floors and in containers. Repair on the derelict (faster component collection) or stockpile components and repair safely on your ship.
- **Mass always matters:** every item aboard adds mass → slower acceleration/deceleration when leaving a stripped derelict. Watch the **accelerometer** and add thrusters (too many is almost never bad). Use the **throttle** to reduce thrust when empty, increase when loaded or for emergency evasion.

### 3.3 Selling Salvage
- Sell at various vendors; **kiosks buy different items and pay different prices**.
- Set up a **barter Zone** on your ship so you don't have to carry each item to the seller. (Barter zones don't work from a stolen ship – see ostranauts-ship-ownership.md.)
- Legal: **License Kiosk**, **Scrap Kiosk** (Port Azikiwe and the Flotilla), **Self Care Kiosk**.
- Black Market: **The Fixer**, **The Broker**.

## 4 Value/Space Cheat Sheet (SPOILERS)
- Early problem: **storage space**. Later (bigger ship): **mass** → more fuel and thrusters needed. Leave room and fuel for maneuvers to avoid "crashing".
- **Base Value** = value when fully repaired (visible with **Business Admin** skill). Actual sell price is lower depending on the buyer.
- **Size** = tiles the object occupies. Not all items listed, only common ones.
- Parts and scrap have low value → keep a small stockpile of everything to repair damaged salvage.

### Quick takeaways (derived)
- Best $/tile: VDX-01 transponder (risky, unrepairable), Hydra, Weber Laser Torch + battery, Bingham-12 EVA suit/helmet, Towing Brace, Halvorson RCS Thrusters, Sulaiman fusion parts.
- Skip when space is tight: chairs, dining tables, stools, passenger seats, TVs, bar tables (< $125/tile).
- Always check sinks for water (and alcohol) and gas canisters for contents.
