## 1 Overview
Owning a ship (almost any kind) is a core element of the game: it gives the freedom to explore local and later inter-planetary space.

## 2 Ship Ownership Basics
- **Full ownership** = buying the ship or derelict from the **Ship Broker** in the **Commercial District** (Azikiwe Commercial). This registers the character as owner in the **OGSIO** ship registration lists.
- Legal ownership alone isn't enough for the ship to be correctly identifiable. It also needs:
  - **XPDR Transponder**: provides uniquely identifiable ship registration information.
  - **Antenna**: required to broadcast the transponder information.
- Both are **required to dock at certain stations**.
- Ships without them in local ATC space may get extra scrutiny from **Security Services (N.A.S.S.)**.

## 3 Less than Legal Ship Ownership (faked transponder)
For players who won't or can't buy a ship/derelict the normal way:
1. Pick a likely ship or derelict.
2. Buy a **replacement transponder** for a ship you legally own, at the Ship Broker.
3. Remove all existing transponders from the target and install the replacement.
4. Once powered up, the target ship shows the registration info of your originally owned ship.

Limitations (currently no way to get full ownership this way):
- Flyable, but **not owned** by the player character.
- **Cannot be sold.**
- **PASS** service can't be used to travel to the ship.
- **Barter zones don't work** from a stolen ship.

Related: in character creation, the Poker Game choice "Grab the Key and Run" gives a stolen Volatile without legal ownership (see ostranauts-character-creation.md, 2.1.7).
