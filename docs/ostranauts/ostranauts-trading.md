### 2.3 Salvage Permit
- The **licensed trade kiosk on K-Leg** (License Kiosk) requires an **active salvage permit to sell**.
- The same permit lets you salvage derelicts in the Boneyard without being harassed and fined by **NASS** officers.
- Abandoned permits can be found on derelicts; they may or may not be expired.
- Item name: "(tier) Ayotimiwa Salvage Permit".

| Tier | Cost | Duration | $/day |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow | $5,000 | 24 hours | $5,000 |
| Red | $13,500 | 72 hours | $4,500 |
| Blue | $30,000 | 7 days | $4,285.71 |
| Silver | $120,000 | 30 days | $4,000 |
| Black | $1,000,000 | 300 days | $3,333.33 |

## 4 Tips for Trading
- Vendors with **better prices** who buy **high-value goods** usually have some **requirement** to trade with them (e.g. the License Kiosk needs a salvage permit).
- Different traders buy different things → **shop around** for the best prices.
- The **Send Item to Barter Zone** toggle requires a **Barter Zone** set up on your ship.
- Selling from a barter zone **may carry a hidden fee** → sometimes less money than bringing the item directly to the buyer. Currently only confirmed for **Cargo Pods sold to the Cargo Kiosk**.

## 5 Bulk Trading
(Main article: Bulk Trading)
- Carry large quantities of trade goods between stations and sell for profit. Goods are **abstract**, not physical inventory items.
- Requirements:
  - At least one **cargo pod** installed (buy at a **Cargo Kiosk**, or rare salvage from derelicts).
  - A ship equipped for **long-distance travel**.
- Four cargo pod types (some cargo needs special transport):
  | Pod type | Protection |
  |---|---|
  | Standard | none |
  | Climate-Controlled | climate control |
  | Radiation-Shielded | radiation shielding |
  | Specialty | both radiation-shielded and climate-controlled |
- When equipped, buy trade goods at a **Cargo Kiosk**.
